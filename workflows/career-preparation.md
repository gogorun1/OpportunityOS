# Career matching and packet preparation

1. Read confirmed personal facts, claim evidence, application history, and exclusions. Search the tracker before evaluating or preparing a role.
2. Verify the job is open on an official page. Capture title, location, responsibilities, start/duration, language, compensation units, academic/work-authorization requirements, and deadline with timezone where available.
3. Separate eligibility from fit. Classify each condition as hard requirement, preference, or unknown. Search for employer evidence before asking the user for facts only they can supply.
4. Match actual duties to demonstrated work, not just title or keywords. Identify missing evidence and mentorship expectations. Preserve contribution boundaries for team projects.
5. Complete a small role map: strongest role family, matching tasks, weak matches, and a contextual contact route if useful. Recommend only roles that survive the user's filters.
6. For a user-selected role, prepare a versioned packet under private/applications/: CV source, cover letter if useful, exact form answers, attachments, destination, consent choices, claim provenance, and an approval checklist.
7. Archive Prepared or Awaiting Approval. Do not submit or contact anyone during preparation. Follow approval-and-execution.md for a later authorized action.

Avoid duplicate applications, repeated outreach, and rewriting historical submitted packets. After a rejection distinguish specific feedback, eligibility evidence, templates, and unknown causes. A generic rejection does not prove a capability gap. Improve future matching with evidence, and propose larger changes for user review.

Human Action is selecting a role, approving a finished packet, attending an interview, practicing a scoped skill, or choosing a contact action. Company/JD research and drafting belong to the agent.
