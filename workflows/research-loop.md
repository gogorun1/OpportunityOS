# Decision-focused research loop

## Control gate

1. Read the live profile, lane priorities, relevant prior briefs, signals, and run memory.
2. Search for the topic and synonyms before selecting work. Use a stable dedup key such as `lane:company:topic` and an event/date suffix only for a distinct delta.
3. Classify the task: **Full Brief** (no reliable prior packet), **Delta Refresh** (material fresh evidence), **Queue Only** (waiting for a catalyst), or **Skipped** (old conclusion remains valid).
4. For a delta, load only a compact context pack: prior thesis, action, assumptions, falsification triggers, and next checkpoint.
5. Fetch the fresh source pack needed to resolve the decision. Prefer official filings, company/product documentation, employer job pages, regulatory publications, and direct customer evidence. Record dates and conflicting claims.
6. Complete one highest-value agent-doable task. Do not manufacture a long report when no decision-relevant delta exists.

## Research method

Reclassify the company or opportunity by what it actually controls. Map suppliers, customers, substitutes, complementary products, distribution, constraints, and feedback loops. Ask what becomes scarce and which financial line or user behavior captures that scarcity.

Break products and segments apart. For company/investment work inspect revenue quality, margins, cash conversion, customer concentration, capex, commitments, financing, dilution, management execution, valuation assumptions, and cycle exposure. For career work inspect responsibilities, junior support, eligibility, language, timing, and evidence fit.

Diagnose competing explanations. A product launch is not customer adoption; backlog is not revenue or cash; a prestigious employer is not a suitable role. Write bear/base/bull or equivalent alternative scenarios and explicit falsification triggers.

If the user has configured analyst/newsletter sources, check fresh public material and report **Source Delta**: new stance, agreement, qualifications, missing variables, confidence, and decision effect. Treat analysts as research leads, not proof. Respect access limits. No particular analyst is required by this template.

## Completed packet

- Agent Completed: concrete work finished and completion date.
- One-line Thesis: the result in plain language.
- Prior Work Check and State Classification.
- What You May Not Know: the non-obvious evidence or mechanism.
- Evidence / Interpretation / Unknowns: clearly separated, with direct citations.
- Reclassification, System Map, Scarcity Test, Product Map, Economic Engine: proportional to the topic; mark irrelevant sections as such.
- What changed / What did not / Action changed? / Assumptions or price zones changed? / Human action changed? for a delta.
- Why This Changes the Decision.
- Portfolio Impact when relevant, based only on the user's actual configured holdings and watchlist.
- Action Label, Confidence, Signal Strength (1–5 with rationale).
- Price Zones / Buy-zone Verdict for investment research, or Insufficient Evidence.
- Bear / Base / Bull and Invalidation / Falsification Triggers.
- Next Checkpoint: observable event, date if verified, and the agent's next task.
- Human Action and Decision Needed: “None now” is valid.
- Source Delta when configured; Experimental Method Used, if any.
- Sources: title, direct URL, publication/event date, access date.

## Investment valuation discipline

Use investment analysis only when that lane is enabled. Do not infer risk tolerance, position size, cost basis, or trading authority. Separate business quality from price attractiveness.

Price zones need a reproducible bridge from revenue, sustainable margins, tax, share count/dilution, debt/cash, and EPS or FCF/share to the valuation. For example, show scenario EPS × justified multiple = implied price. Label assumptions and time horizon. Verify quote timestamp, currency, and ADR/split basis. Use Insufficient Evidence when the bridge is missing; an analyst target or an arbitrary EPS assumption is insufficient. This system never executes trades.

## Archive and continuity

Mark a finished brief Done; keep Sent Email false. Add up to three signals and three networking targets only if justified; prefer updates over duplicates. Record private run memory: timestamp, topic, state, evidence added, result, archive IDs, failure/reconciliation state, and exact reopening trigger. Unavailable tools do not justify claiming success.
