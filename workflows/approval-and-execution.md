# Final-version approval

All emails, outreach, applications, registrations, credit requests, and external form submissions require explicit approval for the exact action and final version. A user may expressly waive approval for a clearly defined scope; do not infer or extend a waiver.

The initial request to prepare or apply, a connected account, a recurring task, a historical approval, and an agent-generated “approved” field do not replace final-version approval.

## Review record

Before asking for approval, finish and show:

- Action type and exact recipient, URL, or destination.
- Final subject/body or all form answers.
- Attachment names and exact versions.
- Consent, subscription, sharing, and payment choices.
- Material unknowns that prevent truthful execution.

Save a private version identifier and content hash when possible. Record the user's exact approval or waiver text, where it came from, its timestamp, version, and scope. Missing facts cannot be supplied by approval.

## Execution boundary

Execute only if the workflow includes execution and the approval applies. Research/background preparation workflows stop at the review packet even when a future send is proposed. If recipient, content, attachments, material answers, or consent choices change, obtain fresh approval unless an explicit waiver covers the change.

After execution, verify the receipt or actual sent item before marking Sent/Submitted. Save confirmation privately. If the outcome is ambiguous, check read-only for success before retrying; avoid duplicate sends and submissions.

Daily Radar email drafts remain Sent Email=false and Delivery Reason=Awaiting human approval until approved and actually sent. Inbox-only delivery never sets Sent Email=true. Keep a maximum of one daily digest email.

Connecting accounts, making trades, spending money, scheduling tasks, and changing sharing permissions have their own user authorization boundaries. Approval to send a message does not authorize those other actions.
